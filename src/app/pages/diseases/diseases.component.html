Normal Retina
Healthy Status

A normal OCT scan shows:

  • Clear, well-defined retinal layers
  • Smooth foveal contour
  • No fluid accumulation
  • Normal retinal thickness
  • Intact photoreceptor layer
Regular eye checkups are recommended even with healthy retinas.
Choroidal Neovascularization (CNV)
Urgent Attention Required

CNV is characterized by:

  • Abnormal blood vessel growth
  • Subretinal fluid accumulation
  • Pigment epithelial detachment
  • Retinal distortion
  • Risk of permanent vision loss
Immediate consultation with a retina specialist is recommended.
Diabetic Macular Edema (DME)
Diabetic Complication

DME is characterized by:

  • Macular thickening
  • Intraretinal fluid cysts
  • Hard exudates
  • Disrupted retinal architecture
  • Central vision impairment
Blood sugar control is essential. Consult your ophthalmologist and endocrinologist.
Drusen (Early AMD)
Early Warning Sign

Drusen indicates:

  • Yellow lipid deposits under retina
  • Early age-related macular degeneration
  • RPE abnormalities
  • Risk of progression to wet AMD
  • Need for regular monitoring
Lifestyle changes and supplements may help slow progression. Regular monitoring advised.
